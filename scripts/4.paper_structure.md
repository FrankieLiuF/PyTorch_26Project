"To leverage the spatial feature extraction capabilities of deep convolutional architectures like ResNet50, we adopted One-Hot Encoding for categorical features. Unlike Label Encoding, which introduces an artificial ordinal relationship and non-uniform pixel intensity gradients, One-Hot Encoding projects tabular data into a higher-dimensional space where each feature is represented as a distinct binary channel. This enhances the contrast in the transformed feature images, allowing the pre-trained CNNs to capture categorical patterns more effectively through local edge and texture detection."
（为了利用 ResNet50 等深度卷积架构的空间特征提取能力，我们对分类特征采用了 One-Hot 编码。与引入人为序数关系和非均匀像素强度梯度的标签编码不同，One-Hot 编码将表格数据投影到更高维的空间，其中每个特征表示为独立的二进制通道。这增强了转换后的特征图像的对比度，使预训练的 CNN 能通过局部边缘和纹理检测更有效地捕捉分类模式。）
